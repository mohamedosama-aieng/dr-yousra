# Section 5 — المحور الخامس: تسعير الشنط و التيشرتات

This section covers the fifth and final content axis of the course: pricing strategy for handmade printed bags and T-shirts. It provides a straightforward, fair pricing method to help students turn their craft into a profitable business.

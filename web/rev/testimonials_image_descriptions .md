- **Platform sources:** WhatsApp (private chats and group chats), Facebook comments, Google Reviews.
- **Badge styles used across all images:**
  - Purple/lavender badge on peach background → online course testimonials
  - Dark magenta/pink badge on gray/dark background → newer batch (in-person + mixed)
- **Common praise themes across all 9 images:**
  - Simple, clear, and heart-reaching explanation style
  - Detailed, comprehensive course content
  - Warm and caring personality / hospitality
  - High information value
  - Motivating and enthusiastic teaching
  - 5-star / 10-out-of-10 ratings
  - Students intend to return for more courses
  - Practical results: students made products (bags, boxes, fabric art) by themselves after the course

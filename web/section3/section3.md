# Section 3 — المحور التقني: استخدام الـ AI

This section is the technical/digital axis of the course. It focuses on using AI tools and free apps (ChatGPT, Canva, Pinterest, Shuttle) to generate, source, and enhance product photos — turning any design into a sales-ready image and boosting the student's ability to sell their handmade products online.

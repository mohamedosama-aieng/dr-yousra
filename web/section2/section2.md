# Section 2 — المحور الثاني (العملي): طباعة التيشرتات والشنط

This section is the hands-on practical axis of the course. It covers multiple techniques for printing on T-shirts and tote bags at home, including DTF transfers, heat vinyl, stencils, embroidery paste, and gold foil — all at minimal cost.
